At IEXE Tec I led UX and front-end development for the redesign of the e-learning platform, using user-centered design principles, service design, design thinking and analytical UX methods. I conducted UX research and user interviews, designed the user experience (user flows, visual styles, wireframes and prototypes) and a design system for IEXE Tec.
