Visual identity and communication pieces for the first _Jornadas por la Paz_ at UAM (September 18–22, 2023), a week of conferences, workshops and conversations organized with ANUIES and the Research Network on Culture of Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions.

The event reuses the Morse-code language of the Culture of Peace program: the word _peace_ drawn in dots and dashes frames the title, next to a dove that carries the colors of the institutional palette. The system covers the general poster, daily programs, social media carousels, announcements for each conference and workshop, and screens for the university's displays.
