Mi Casa Resource Center (MCRC), in Denver, Colorado, needed to migrate its Salesforce org to the Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP) and, alongside it, a new data model that fit the way the organization works.

## The objective

Restructure the Salesforce database from scratch to get cleaner results and data points when reporting to the government (SBA) and to grants (DEDO, Westwood, and others).

## How we worked

The migration project was a team of four, led by me.

1. **Implementation plan.** Project management and clarifying scope; mapping the data architecture; defining a data migration plan; mapping recurrent and critical org processes; setting up environments; creating an organizational change management plan.
2. **Configuration.** Installing and configuring NPSP in a sandbox; configuring data and processes (fields, flows and automations); building their Educational, Reports and NPSP apps with their own automation, fields and processes.
3. **Analyzing data quality.** Reviewing existing data integrity issues; data quality testing; a data quality action plan for after the migration.
4. **Testing.**
5. **Migration and transition.**
