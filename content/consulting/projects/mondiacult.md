Design for the international seminar _Cultural diversity and media_, UAM's side event at MONDIACULT 2022, the UNESCO world conference on cultural policies, held in Mexico City on September 26, 2022.

A colorful, organic shape language drawn from the MONDIACULT 2022 identity runs through the whole system: posters, social media and TV programs, speaker panels, banners, and printed badges.
