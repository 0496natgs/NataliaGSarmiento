What does the client want to communicate with the new brand?

One of the essential purposes of Accessalud is to return Accessalud is driven by the ideology that health is not a business. As such, it goes against the status quo of most health care services today, to the paradigm where the patient is the primary interest of health care, not money.

Accessalud opens its doors to anyone who needs state-of-the-art medical care and wants patients to perceive Accessalud as a revolutionary medical institution in its health services and patient care.

In a way, we wanted to return to the medical "sanatorium" model, but with the technology and medical advances of the 21st century. Dr. Tamayo encompasses these aspects. Accessalud has to communicate these characteristics of Dr. Tamayo to patients to continue his legacy.

How will the marketing strategy be developed?

The marketing strategy to be used is designed to achieve the objectives set out above, which are designed to integrate Behavioral Change Design. In the case of Accessalud, it is planned to use social networks to support the marketing and promotion objectives. The social networks used are Facebook, Instagram, Twitter and Medium, the latter intended for informative blog posts. Three behaviors to be modified were identified, which were linked to the aforementioned objectives.
