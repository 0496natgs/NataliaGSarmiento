The UAM Culture of Peace Microsite was designed as a space where you can find information about the actions for Peace that UAM carries out in collaboration with ANUIES, other allied institutions, and collaborators. The logo of the UAM Culture of Peace program is made up of the word peace in Morse code since it is a universal and visual language that is part of each of the logos of each section to create unity in the design and unity with the word Peace.

The color palette took into account the institutional colors of the UAM, such as black and turquoise, that are found on the site of the same to maintain its presence in the design; likewise, it was designed with many white spaces to connote a concept of peace and unity between the contents presented.

The fonts used were Gandhi and Helvetica, the first is the font of the microsite identity, and the second is the institutional font of the Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana; the page aims to create a repository of content, resources, links, and news among others, that can be consulted at any time.

The repository contains the 4 sections into which the microsite is divided, Building a Culture of Peace, Educating for Peace, Communicating for Peace, and Resources and Documents, which can be found on the main page. Each contains its content repository that together makes up the entire microsite.
