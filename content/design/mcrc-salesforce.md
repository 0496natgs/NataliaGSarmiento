The project was held for Mi Casa Resource Center (MCRC). And it was a Migration Project to migrate their Salesforce Org to the Nonprofit Sucess Pack of Salesforce and create with conjunction a new Data Model that could adapt to their needs as an organization.

The following image shows the Data Architecture Design of the new Data Model of Mi Casa Resource Center, which joined the Salesforce packages to migrate, and the new data model built from scratch, with all the relations needed to consider before migrating.

The main objective was to restructure their Salesforce Database from scratch to accomplish cleaner results and data points on their Data when reporting to the Government (SBA) and Grants (DEDO, Westwood, etc.)

The Migration Project was made up of a team of four people, led by me, in which we followed the following steps:

1. Implementation Plan

- Project management and clarification of certain scopes

- Mapping the data architecture

- Defining a Data Migration Plan

- Creating maps of recurrent and critical Org Processes

- Setting up environments

- Creating an organizational Change Management Plan

2. Configuration

- Install and configure the NPSP in Sandbox

- Configuration of data and processes (Creation, deletion, and update on fields, Flows, and processes in Salesforce)

- Creation of their Educational, Reports, and NPSP Apps in Salesforce with their respective configuration of Automation, fields, and processes

3. Analyzing Data Quality

- Review of existing data integrity issues

- Data quality testing

- Data quality action plan after migration

4. Testing

5. Migration & Transition
