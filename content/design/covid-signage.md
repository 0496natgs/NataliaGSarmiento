Generate a whole Signage System for COVID-19 to be implemented in the University (UAM-C). The system was designed with the COVID-19 Design Toolkit (Version 1.0 US - October 2020) as the base.

The system was designed to engage people to get their attention, make the virus visible, make the right information at the right time, and coordinate it across the installations of the University.

The approach to design the signals was to use the "Risk Framework" established in the COVID-19 Design Toolkit to identify what information is needed in people's journey across the University. There were 2 types of signals designed for this project (fast information and slow information).

The whole system required a set of icons designed according to the guidelines of the University signage.
