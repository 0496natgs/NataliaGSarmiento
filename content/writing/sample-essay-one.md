## What this page shows

This is placeholder text for an essay. Tags appear as small chips under the title, and the category appears as a lime badge, as on the portfolio index.[^1]

On a wide screen, a footnote also appears in the margin beside the line that cites it, so the reader never has to jump to the bottom of the page.[^2]

## Using it

Add `cover: /static/your-image.png` to a piece's frontmatter to show an image on its card instead of the coloured poster.

[^1]: This is a footnote. Write `[^1]` in the text and `[^1]: your note` at the end of the page.
