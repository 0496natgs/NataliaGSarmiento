_A placeholder collection. Each poem has its own title, and the page keeps every line break._

### I. The window

This is where the first poem goes.
Each line break is kept,
and the column stays narrow
so the lines can breathe.

### II. The table

Put the second poem here,
with its own title above it,
and a little space
between one poem and the next.

### III. The door

The third poem closes the collection.
Add as many as you need:
a collection can be three poems,
or thirty.
