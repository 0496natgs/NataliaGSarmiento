## My stance

Everything I write here, essays, stories, poems, flash, notes and newsletters, is written by me. I do not use AI to write, to rewrite, to edit or to translate my writing. As a writer and an artist, I want the thinking, the voice and the craft to stay in my own hands.

The same goes for the art: my visual notes, illustrations, photographs and design work are made by me, without generative AI.

## Where AI was used

The website itself. I built this site with the help of an AI coding assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), which wrote the code, the layout and the structure of the pages. It also helped draft the interface text of the site (menus, buttons, labels), the short descriptions on some project pages, and the Spanish version of that text.

That is the plumbing around the work, not the work.
