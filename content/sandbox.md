---

Templates and placeholders live here. They are hidden from the live site: they are not in the menu, the section lists, the home page, search, the graph, the sitemap or the RSS feed. Only someone with the link can open them, and each one opens in its real layout (poem, essay, photograph, video) so you can test it.

**To try something new:** add these two lines to the page's front matter (between the `---` lines).

```yaml
sandbox: true
unlisted: true
```

**To publish it:** delete those two lines. The page then appears in its section on the live site.
